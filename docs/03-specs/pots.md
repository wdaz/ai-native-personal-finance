## 1. Purpose

The user keeps money aside in pots: each pot has a name, a theme colour, a target and the total saved so far. The page lists every pot with its progress, creates, edits and deletes pots, and moves money between the account's Current Balance and a pot. Money is conserved: a deposit takes it from the balance, a withdrawal and a pot's deletion give it back (`write-path.md` 2.8), so Overview's Current Balance follows every move (US-04 AC2). An AI agent does the same through six page-scoped tools that use the same routes and, for a deletion, the same on-screen confirmation.

**2.3 Percentage and bar** (US-21 AC1, US-23 AC2, US-25 AC3; R-08). `potPercent(total, target)` is `total / target × 100` rounded half up to two decimals, computed **in integers** as basis points (hundredths of a percent): `⌊(2 · total · 10,000 + target) / (2 · target)⌋`, with `BigInt` or within `Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER` (the largest numerator, 2 · 575,600 · 10,000 + 99,999,999,999, is 111,511,999,999, about 1.1 × 10¹¹, printed by the figures script). Floating point is not used: `(total / target * 100).toFixed(2)` rounds some ties down (4.4). `formatPercent` writes the basis points with exactly two decimals and no thousands separator ("7.95%", "106.67%", "57560000.00%"). A target is at least 1 cent (`write-path.md` 4.1), so the division is defined. The percentage shows the real value, above 100 % when the total passes the target; the **bar** is capped: `potFill = min(10,000, potPercent)` basis points, so it is full at 100 % and beyond. A total of 0 shows "0.00%" and an empty track.

A readable amount is **clamped as the design clamps it** (`Math.min(amt, s.balance)`, `Math.min(amt, pot.total)`): an addition moves `min(amount, balance)`, a withdrawal `min(amount, total)` — the preview never shows a total the confirm could not reach, while the field's message says why the amount is refused. The clamp is the preview's only: the confirm refuses the amount (the amount field below) — the designer's changelog §19i (PO-9: "Add Money above the current balance and Withdraw above the pot total are refused with the approved messages (US-25/26 AC2); the preview still clamps"; before, the drawing clamped the amount silently; drawing pending).
  - New total = total ± moved. The staying segment is `potFill(min(total, new total), target)` wide; the moving one `min(10,000 − staying, potFill(moved, target))` basis points wide, so the two together never pass the track (US-25 AC3: "the preview and the bar cap at 100 % while the percentage text shows the real value"); the percentage is `potPercent(new total, target)`.
  - It updates as the person types (a change of the input's text, not a check: it shows no message, `ui-kit.md` 2.5; `MoneyModal` reads the text from the `input` events that bubble to its `<form>`, as `PotForm`'s counter does, 2.5) and is not a live region; it is text a screen reader reads in order before the field. There is no separate current-total line (PO-Q5 (a), the owner's answer): "New Amount" shows the current total when the modal opens, and the bar's dark segment keeps it while the person types, so US-25 AC1 ("shows current total") is read as met, as drawn.
  - Before anything is typed the new total is the current total, the moving segment is empty and the percentage is the card's.
- **The amount field** (`AmountField`, `ui-kit.md` 2.5; placeholder "e.g. 2000", the design's — the same as the target's; `type="text"` with `inputMode="decimal"`, `ui-kit.md` 2.5, and the design's since the designer's changelog §19j, PO-10, which covers the Pots and Budgets modals, drawing pending): the four messages of `parseAmountInput` — the one shared amount check of `write-path.md` 2.7, the designer's changelog §22a (WP-1: empty → "Can't be empty"; not whole cents → "Enter an amount with up to two decimals"; 0 or less → "Amount must be greater than 0"; above $999,999,999.99 → "Amount is too large"; it applies after PO-10, and "the PO-9 balance checks run after these shape checks") — then the page's rule (`ui-kit.md` 2.5: "the page adds its own rules after these"): add — more than the page's balance → **"Amount exceeds your current balance"** (`exceeds_balance`, US-25 AC2); withdraw — more than the pot's total → **"Amount exceeds this pot's total"** (`exceeds_total`, US-26 AC2) — the approved messages §19i (PO-9) names. An amount equal to the limit is allowed (4.3, 4.5). The balance the page checks against is the one it last read (2.8); the server checks again inside its transaction (`write-path.md` 2.8), so a balance changed in another tab comes back as a server `exceeds_balance` under the field.
- **At a $0.00 balance** (US-25 AC2: "Given Current Balance is $0.00, the field shows that message on any input" — `ui-kit.md` 2.5 asks this spec to say how it reads it). Read here with `ui-kit.md` 2.5's order and timing: whenever the field is checked (its blur once touched, or submit), an empty field shows "Can't be empty", text that is not an amount "Enter an amount with up to two decimals", 0 or a negative amount "Amount must be greater than 0", an amount above the maximum "Amount is too large", and **every amount that passes those four shows "Amount exceeds your current balance"** — so every amount the person could mean is refused with the balance message, and nothing at all can be added. The "+ Add Money" button stays enabled and the modal opens. This reading is the owner's answer to **PO-Q2 (a)** (the fixed order; the server checks in the same order, `write-path.md` 2.7). The mirror for a pot whose total is $0.00: every amount is "Amount exceeds this pot's total".
- **Submit** (`ui-kit.md` 2.7): `POST /api/pots/:id/deposit` or `…/withdraw` `{ amount }` (cents). **200:** the modal closes, focus returns to the card's button, and once 2.8's refresh lands the card shows the new total, percentage and bar and the page's balance is the database's (2.8; US-25 AC3: the amount is deducted from Current Balance; US-26 AC3: added to it). **400** `exceeds_balance` / `exceeds_total` / an amount code: under the field; an `exceeds_balance` or `exceeds_total` also calls `router.refresh()` (2.8), so the balance and the total the field and the preview check against become the database's, and the next blur does not clear a message that is still true. **404** (the pot was deleted meanwhile): `ui-kit.md` 2.7, the notice "This pot no longer exists". The rest: `ui-kit.md` 2.7.

**2.8 After a write — the page, Overview and the agent** (US-04 AC2, US-36 AC1, US-40 AC3).
- **The page's own writes** — the pattern `budgets.md` 2.9 gives Budgets, so the two pages refresh the same way. `PotsBoard` owns one write function used by the pot forms, the money modals and the delete dialog: it counts its requests in and out of the `ModalSlot` (`trackWrite`, `ui-kit.md` 2.2), sends through `apiSend` (`write-path.md` 2.11 (2); with `X-Via: webmcp` only when the delete was a tool's, `ui-kit.md` 2.3 item 5) and maps the answer. On a success (201, 200, 204) the modal closes (focus as 2.5–2.7 say), any "no longer exists" notice is cleared (`ui-kit.md` 2.9), and the page calls `router.refresh()` inside `startTransition`, so the server component reads `getPots` again and the grid, the balance and the "Already used" options show the new state **with no reload** (US-22 AC3, US-23 AC1, US-24 AC1, US-25 AC3, US-26 AC3). The page does not apply the answer's body itself: what it renders is always the server's last read, a deletion's refund included (it computes no money). While that refresh is pending the grid carries `aria-busy="true"`; the previous state stays until the new one arrives (no skeleton). The balance a money modal checks against is therefore the database's after every write the page made; a modal opened before the refresh lands checks against the previous read, and the server checks again (`write-path.md` 2.8; the next bullet).
- **A refusal that shows the page's data is old.** A 400 carrying `taken` (2.5) or `exceeds_balance` / `exceeds_total` (2.6) means another tab or an agent changed the data since the page read it: the page puts the message under its field and runs the refresh of a success (in `startTransition`, `aria-busy` on the grid), so the client checks of the next blur and submit run on the new read; the modal stays open with what was typed (as below).
- **Overview.** Overview's server component calls `getOverview` directly (`overview.md` 2.1), and every app page renders per request — `app/(app)/layout.tsx` calls `connection()` (ADR-0006; `tests/api/app-pages.spec.ts` fails when that is gone) — so after any pot write its Current Balance and Pots card show the change when the person opens it again (US-04 AC2), with no step of this page. Income and Expenses never change (US-04 AC3); a budget write never changes the balance (`write-path.md` 2.8).
- **The agent's writes** (US-40 AC3: "visible in the UI immediately (same state as a user action)"). `add_pot`, `edit_pot`, `add_money_to_pot` and `withdraw_from_pot` call the API directly (2.13), not through the page. A tool's `execute` is a module-level function (`PAGE_TOOLS`, `src/webmcp/tools/registry.ts`) and cannot call a React hook, so the refresh is added where a hook runs: `PotsTools` (the client component the layout renders, as `OverviewTools` is) calls `useRouter()` and builds, once per router (`useMemo`), the list it hands to `WebMcpTools` (`src/webmcp/WebMcpTools.tsx`) — `PAGE_TOOLS.pots` with the `execute` of `add_pot`, `edit_pot`, `add_money_to_pot` and `withdraw_from_pot` wrapped so that, after a result without `isError`, it calls `router.refresh()` and returns the result unchanged; `list_pots` and `delete_pot` are passed as they are. The registry keeps the unwrapped definitions, so `tests/unit/webmcp/registry.test.ts` checks the same names, schemas and annotations; the memoised list keeps `WebMcpTools`' effect (`[tools]`) from registering again on every render. The refresh makes the Server Component read `getPots` again and `PotsBoard` renders the new props, as after its own write; it runs outside `PotsBoard`'s transition, so the grid is not marked `aria-busy` for an agent's write (the `aria-busy` above is the page's own writes'). This is the mechanism `budgets.md` 2.13 states for Budgets (`BudgetsTools`, `router.refresh()` after a tool's success), stated here identically; `src/webmcp/bus.ts` stays as `ui-kit.md` 2.3 defines it, with no `notifyWrite`/`onWrite` (v0.3 and v0.4 of this spec had added them, which `ui-kit.md` 2.3 and §6 do not define), so `ui-kit.md` 2.3 needs no amendment. `delete_pot` needs none of this: it goes through the page's own dialog and write function (`ui-kit.md` 2.3 item 5), whose refresh is the page's. Until the refresh has landed, the page does not show a pot an agent has just added, so a `delete_pot` of its id in that moment answers `not_found` (`ui-kit.md` 2.3 item 3: an id the page does not show), with no dialog and no request; once the card is shown, the same call opens the dialog.
- **A tool write while a modal is open.** The refresh re-reads the records under the open modal; the modal stays open with what the person typed, and its preview and its "Already used" options follow the new read. A tool's write does not claim the `ModalSlot` (only a dialog opener does, `ui-kit.md` 2.2) and is not counted by it (it is not the page's write function); a person's submit that then collides answers `taken`, `exceeds_balance` or `exceeds_total` (the bullet above).

**2.12 The API** (`write-path.md` 2.1's Pots routes; every write follows its 2.2 order, answers 2.6, validation 2.7, consistency 2.8, threshold 2.9, rate limit 2.10; `GET` and `POST /api/pots` share `app/api/pots/route.ts`, `PATCH` and `DELETE` `app/api/pots/[id]/route.ts`, the moves `app/api/pots/[id]/deposit/route.ts` and `…/withdraw/route.ts`).

```
PotDto  = { id: uuid, name: string (1–30, trimmed), theme: one of the 15 themes,
            target: integer cents (1–99,999,999,999), total: integer cents ≥ 0,
            percentBasisPoints: integer ≥ 0 }            // potPercent(total, target), 2.3; 795 means 7.95 %
PotsDto = { balance: { current: integer cents ≥ 0 },      // Current Balance: what a deposit may not exceed
            items: PotDto[] }                              // creation order, at most 15
```
The schemas are named noun first, as `src/shared/schemas.ts` names its own (`LoginSchema`, `OverviewDtoSchema`) and as `budgets.md` 2.10 names `BudgetCreateSchema` and `BudgetEditSchema`. Every write answer wraps the record under its noun (`{ pot }`, and `{ pot, balance }` for a move, which must carry two parts), so a create, an edit and a move read the pot from the same key.
No sum of the pots' totals: the page does not show one (§8; Overview's "Total Saved" is `overview.md`'s), so neither does `list_pots` (2.13).
Both are `z.strictObject` (`src/shared/schemas.ts`), so a leaked field (`seq`, `seeded`, `createdAt`) fails an API test.

| Route | Body (shared schema) | Success | Business-rule 400s (`write-path.md` 2.7) | 404 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `GET /api/pots` | — | 200 `PotsDto` | — | — |
| `POST /api/pots` | `PotCreateSchema { name, target, theme }` | 201 `{ pot: PotDto }` with `total: 0` (US-22 AC3) | `name` `taken`; `theme` `taken` | — |
| `PATCH /api/pots/:id` | `PotUpdateSchema { name, target, theme }` — **all three required**, as the form sends them: an absent or `null` field is `required` (`write-path.md` 2.7's table), and `{}` is `required` on each of the three (`write-path.md` 4.4); partial edits are out of scope (§8), as on Budgets (`budgets.md` 2.10) | 200 `{ pot: PotDto }`, `total` unchanged | `name` `taken` by another pot (its own name passes, US-23 AC1); `theme` `taken` by another pot | the pot is gone |

- **Fields** (hand-off H1; NFR-S3): `name` — a string, trimmed, 1–30 UTF-16 code units (`write-path.md` 2.7's mapper pairs: absent, `null`, `""` or only spaces `required`; a non-string `invalid_format`; 31 `too_long`); `target`, `amount` — integer cents, `1 ≤ x ≤ 99,999,999,999` (`write-path.md` 2.7's amount pairs); `theme` — `ThemeSchema` (the documented names, "Navy Grey" with its space); the path `id` — `z.uuid().max(36)` (`write-path.md` 2.2 step 6). A request schema strips unknown keys (`total` in a `POST` or `PATCH` body is dropped: a pot's total changes only by a move).
- **Order inside one request** (`write-path.md` 2.7, 2.8): shape first; then, in the transaction, the pot by `id` (404 when it is gone, before any business rule); then the rules — every `taken` found is reported (a `POST` that takes both a used name and a used theme answers both issues). There is no separate "15 pots" check: the 15 themes are unique among pots, so a 16th pot's theme is always `taken`.
- **Money** (`write-path.md` 2.8, ADR-0005): a deposit is the conditional update pair `Balance.current −= x WHERE current ≥ x` and `Pot.total += x`; a withdrawal `Pot.total −= x WHERE total ≥ x` and `Balance.current += x`; zero rows updated → the transaction rolls back and the answer is `exceeds_balance` / `exceeds_total` (a re-read tells it from a missing pot, 404). `Balance.current + Σ Pot.total` is unchanged by a deposit, a withdrawal and a deletion (4.3). A pot's `total` may exceed its `target` (`data-model.md`); an edit may set the target below the total (US-23 AC2).
- **Every answer** carries `Cache-Control: no-store` and `X-Request-Id` (`write-path.md` 2.2 step 10; `GET /api/pots` sets `no-store` itself, as `transactions.md` 2.13). `GET` changes nothing (`write-path.md` 7.2); a 500 is `{ error: "server_error", message: "The pots are unavailable" }` after `console.error("GET /api/pots failed", error)`.

## 5. Data

Reads `Pot` (`id`, `seq`, `name`, `target`, `total`, `theme`) and `Balance.current`; writes `Pot` (create, edit, delete, `total` by a move) and `Balance.current` (a move and a deletion only, `write-path.md` 2.8); never `income` or `expenses` (US-04 AC3) and never a `Budget` or a `Transaction`. The database is the source of truth; `data.json` is only the seed (US-36 AC2). Money is `BigInt` in the database, integer cents in every body and DTO, text only at the edge (`formatMoney`, `formatPercent`). Ids are server-generated and change on every reset (`write-path.md` 2.8).
